SECTION 1 — DEVICE DESCRIPTION
The FingerSense FS-200 is a reusable, battery-powered fingertip pulse oximeter
that measures functional oxygen saturation (SpO2) and pulse rate by transmission
pulse oximetry at 660 nm (red) and 905 nm (infrared). Maximum optical output
power is below 15 mW. The device is intended for spot-check measurement on adult
and paediatric patients in professional healthcare facilities and in the home
healthcare environment.

Alarm conditions: low SpO2 (high priority), low/high pulse rate (medium

system delay of less than 10 seconds were all verified. Conclusion: PASS.

SECTION 6 — CLINICAL ACCURACY (SpO2)
A controlled desaturation study was performed in accordance with
ISO 80601-2-61:2017 clause 201.12.1.101 on 12 healthy adult volunteers,
yielding 216 paired data points against a CO-oximeter reference over the
saturation range 70 to 100 percent. The subject cohort included 4 subjects
with dark skin pigmentation (Fitzpatrick V-VI), 4 with intermediate and 4
with light pigmentation. Result: SpO2 accuracy root mean square (ARMS) of
1.9 percent over 70 to 100 percent saturation. Bland-Altman analysis is
provided in Annex 6A with a mean bias of -0.3 percent and limits of
agreement of -4.1 to +3.5 percent.

SECTION 7 — SOFTWARE
The embedded firmware is developed under a software lifecycle process
conforming to IEC 62304:2006 + A1:2015. Software safety classification:
Class B, justified on the basis that a software failure could contribute to
a hazardous situation resulting in non-serious injury through a delayed or
erroneous saturation reading. Included: software development plan SDP-FS200;
software requirements specification SRS-FS200 rev 4; software architecture
document; software verification and validation report SVVR-FS200; anomaly
list with residual anomaly justification; and the SOUP inventory (FreeRTOS
10.4.3, CMSIS-DSP 1.9.0) with its associated risk assessment.
