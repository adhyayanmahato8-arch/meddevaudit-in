PRODUCT SUBMISSION DOSSIER
Device: CardioEase CE-9 Automatic Upper Arm Blood Pressure Monitor
Manufacturer: Shenzhen Hualing Electronics Co. Ltd, Guangdong, China

1. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
The CardioEase CE-9 is a fully automatic upper-arm blood pressure monitor
using the oscillometric method. It displays systolic pressure, diastolic
pressure and pulse rate on a backlit LCD, stores 99 readings per user, and is
powered by 4 x AA alkaline batteries. It is intended for home use by adults.

2. REGULATORY STATUS
The device is CE marked and is exported to over 30 countries. The company
declares that the product complies with all applicable international standards
for non-invasive blood pressure monitors. A Declaration of Conformity signed by
the Quality Director of Shenzhen Hualing Electronics Co. Ltd is attached at
Annex 1 confirming that the device is freely sold in China.

3. QUALITY SYSTEM
The manufacturing facility operates a quality management system. An ISO 13485
certificate is held by the company. Certificate details are available on
request.

4. SAFETY TESTING
A test report to IEC 60601-1 general electrical safety was issued by
Guangdong Testing Institute, report number GDT-2021-44120. Conclusion: pass.

An electromagnetic compatibility assessment to IEC 60601-1-2 was carried out.
Emissions were tested to CISPR 11. Immunity was tested at the levels applicable
to the professional healthcare facility environment. Report GDT-2021-44121.

5. PERFORMANCE
The pressure transducer was verified against a reference manometer over the
range 0 to 299 mmHg. Measured cuff pressure accuracy is within plus or minus
3 mmHg across the range, meeting IEC 80601-2-30. Calibration of the reference
manometer is traceable to a national standard. Test report GDT-2021-44122.

The device incorporates an automatic deflation function that releases cuff
pressure at the end of each measurement cycle.

Clinical performance: the oscillometric algorithm is based on a mature
platform used in the company's earlier CE-5 and CE-7 models, which have been
sold since 2016 without reported accuracy complaints.

6. ACCESSORIES
Supplied with a standard adult cuff. A large cuff is available separately.
